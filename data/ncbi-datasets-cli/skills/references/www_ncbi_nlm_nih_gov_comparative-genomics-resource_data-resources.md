# Data resources

## Use the NCBI Toolkit to download data

### Datasets

Easily gather data from across databases using [NCBI Datasets](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/).
Find and download gene, transcript, protein, and genome sequences, annotation, and metadata using the NCBI Datasets
website and [command-line tools](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/docs/v2/download-and-install/).

Citation: [Exploring and retrieving sequence and metadata for species across the tree of life with NCBI Datasets](https://https.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11226681/)

## Use the NCBI Toolkit to explore data

### Gene data

Find nomenclature, reference sequences (RefSeq), maps, pathways, expression, transcript and protein information and
sequences with [Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/).

You can also identify genes with similar functions, including orthologous genes, across a wide range of species
and organisms.

### Genome data

Browse genome data, including genome, transcript, and protein sequences, genome annotation, and metadata for your
favorite organism with the [taxonomy browser](/datasets/taxonomy/tree/).

### Protein data

Find information about proteins and access to compare [Protein Family Models](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/protfam/)
across a database of homologous proteins with similar function, search for
[Conserved Domains](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Structure/cdd/wrpsb.cgi) within a protein or coding nucleotide
sequence, or use [SparcleLabel](https://ftp.ncbi.nih.gov/pub/mmdb/cdd/SparcleLabel/) to perform functional
characterization or naming of larger protein sets.
