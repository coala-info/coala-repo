Program: psmc (Pairwise SMC Model)

Usage:   psmc [options] input.txt

Options: -p STR      pattern of parameters [4+5*3+4]
         -t FLOAT    maximum 2N0 coalescent time [15]

         -S          simulate sequence
         -d          perform decoding
         -D          print full posterior probabilities
